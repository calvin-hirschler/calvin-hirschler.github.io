AI Research Abstract Matcher

01/2025 - 05/2025

A tool used to create a team of researchers based on a sample abstract. Leverages AI to quickly and automatically find researchers with the strengths in the abstract to pair them on a team. Ensures quick, diverse, and optimal team-picking.

Worked on a team under the guidance of the associate dean of research at Auburn University to help create this tool based on a real-world problem. Utilized Hugging Face, Python, and prompt engineering to achieve desirable results. Presented findings in weekly meetings while iterating over the design to meet new goals.

Autonomous Pac-Man

04/2022 - 06/2022

[Image/Video Placeholder]
